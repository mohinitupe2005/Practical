19. Create Instructor collection and execute following queries
a. Delete details of instructor having salary < 30000
b. Find instructors with either salary<50000 or deparment is computer
c. Find out details of instructors who have salary==50000
d. Find employee id, first name, along with salary where salary>50000


Step 1: Create / Select Database
use CollegeDB

🧱 Step 2: Create Collection
db.createCollection("Instructors")

👨‍🏫 Step 3: Insert Sample Data (One by One)
db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 1, name: "Omkar", dept: "IT", salary: 28000})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 2, name: "Saurabh", dept: "Computer", salary: 50000})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 3, name: "Sneha", dept: "Mechanical", salary: 55000})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 4, name: "Rohan", dept: "Civil", salary: 47000})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 5, name: "Pooja", dept: "Computer", salary: 32000})

🔍 Step 4: Execute Queries
a️⃣ Delete details of instructors having salary < 30000
db.Instructors.deleteMany({salary: {$lt: 30000}})


✅ Explanation: Deletes all documents where salary is less than ₹30,000.

b️⃣ Find instructors with either salary < 50000 OR department is “Computer”
db.Instructors.find({$or: [{salary: {$lt: 50000}}, {dept: "Computer"}]})


✅ Explanation: $or combines two conditions; retrieves records matching any one of them.

c️⃣ Find details of instructors who have salary = 50000
db.Instructors.find({salary: 50000})


✅ Explanation: Simple equality condition on the salary field.

d️⃣ Find employee id, name, and salary where salary > 50000
db.Instructors.find({salary: {$gt: 50000}}, {inst_id: 1, name: 1, salary: 1, _id: 0})
