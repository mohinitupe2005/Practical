20. Create Instructor collection and execute following queries
a. Find out details of instructors having salary >40000 and < 80000
b. Display the details of instructors who are living in Maharashtra
c. Find out details of instructors working in AIML department
d. Find instructor id along with department and salary where department
has substring o.


Step 1: Create / Select Database
use CollegeDB

🧱 Step 2: Create Collection
db.createCollection("Instructors")

👨‍🏫 Step 3: Insert Sample Data (One by One)
db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 1, name: "Omkar", dept: "IT", salary: 45000, city: "Pune", state: "Maharashtra"})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 2, name: "Saurabh", dept: "AIML", salary: 70000, city: "Mumbai", state: "Maharashtra"})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 3, name: "Sneha", dept: "Computer", salary: 82000, city: "Delhi", state: "Delhi"})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 4, name: "Rohan", dept: "Mechanical", salary: 38000, city: "Nagpur", state: "Maharashtra"})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 5, name: "Sakshi", dept: "Civil", salary: 50000, city: "Indore", state: "Madhya Pradesh"})

🔍 Step 4: Execute Queries
a️⃣ Find details of instructors having salary > 40000 and < 80000
db.Instructors.find({salary: {$gt: 40000, $lt: 80000}})


✅ Explanation: $gt = greater than, $lt = less than — both conditions must be true.

b️⃣ Display details of instructors who are living in Maharashtra
db.Instructors.find({state: "Maharashtra"})


✅ Explanation: Finds instructors where state field exactly matches “Maharashtra.”

c️⃣ Find details of instructors working in AIML department
db.Instructors.find({dept: "AIML"})


✅ Explanation: Simple equality query on the dept field.

d️⃣ Find instructor ID, department, and salary where department contains substring “o”
db.Instructors.find({dept: /o/i}, {inst_id: 1, dept: 1, salary: 1, _id: 0})
