9. Create a database Execute at least 10 queries on any suitable MongoDB
database that demonstrates following querying techniques:
a. find and findOne (specific values)
b. Query criteria (Query conditionals, OR queries, $not, Conditional
semantics)
c. Type-specific queries (Null, Regular expression, Querying arrays)

Step 1: Create / Select Database
use StudentDB

🧱 Step 2: Create Collection and Insert Sample Data
db.Students.insertMany([
  {roll_no: 1, name: "Omkar", dept: "IT", marks: 85, hobbies: ["Cricket", "Coding"], city: "Pune"},
  {roll_no: 2, name: "Sakshi", dept: "CSE", marks: 92, hobbies: ["Reading"], city: "Mumbai"},
  {roll_no: 3, name: "Rohan", dept: "IT", marks: 55, hobbies: ["Music", "Gaming"], city: "Solapur"},
  {roll_no: 4, name: "Sneha", dept: "ENTC", marks: 60, hobbies: ["Dancing"], city: "Pune"},
  {roll_no: 5, name: "Saurabh", dept: "CSE", marks: null, hobbies: ["Coding"], city: "Nashik"}
])

🔍 Step 3: Execute 10 Queries
1️⃣ find() – Display all students
db.Students.find()

2️⃣ findOne() – Display one record from IT department
db.Students.findOne({dept: "IT"})

3️⃣ Query Criteria – Students with marks greater than 80
db.Students.find({marks: {$gt: 80}})

4️⃣ Query Criteria – Students with marks between 60 and 90
db.Students.find({marks: {$gte: 60, $lte: 90}})

5️⃣ OR Query – Students from IT or CSE departments
db.Students.find({$or: [{dept: "IT"}, {dept: "CSE"}]})

6️⃣ $not Operator – Students not from Pune
db.Students.find({city: {$not: {$eq: "Pune"}}})

7️⃣ Type-Specific (Null) – Students with no marks entered
db.Students.find({marks: {$eq: null}})

8️⃣ Regular Expression – Students whose name starts with “S”
db.Students.find({name: /^S/})

9️⃣ Querying Arrays – Students who have "Coding" as a hobby
db.Students.find({hobbies: "Coding"})

🔟 Querying Arrays – Students having two hobbies
db.Students.find({hobbies: {$size: 2}})
