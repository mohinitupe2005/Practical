🔍 Queries:
1️⃣ Find the record of the student who has got highest marks in DBMS
db.Student.find({subject: "DBMS"}).sort({marks: -1}).limit(1)

2️⃣ Find the average result of TOC subject
db.Student.aggregate([{$match: {subject: "TOC"}}, {$group: {_id: "$subject", avg_marks: {$avg: "$marks"}}}])

3️⃣ Find the record of the student who has got lowest marks in CNT
db.Student.find({subject: "CNT"}).sort({marks: 1}).limit(1)
