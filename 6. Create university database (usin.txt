Create university database (using mongodb)  Find the list of teachers in IT dept.  Find the list of teachers who have salary greater than 50000.  Find the teacher’s list in descending order.  Remove the teacher whose status is not approved.  Give the increment of rs.20000 who has salary less than 30000.


Step 1: Select / Create Database
use UniversityDB

🧱 Step 2: Create Collection
db.createCollection("Teachers")

🧑‍🏫 Step 3: Insert Sample Data (One by One)
db.Teachers.insertOne({t_id: 1, name: "Omkar", dept: "IT", salary: 28000, status: "approved"})

db.Teachers.insertOne({t_id: 2, name: "Sakshi", dept: "IT", salary: 55000, status: "approved"})

db.Teachers.insertOne({t_id: 3, name: "Rohan", dept: "CSE", salary: 48000, status: "approved"})

db.Teachers.insertOne({t_id: 4, name: "Sneha", dept: "ENTC", salary: 60000, status: "approved"})

db.Teachers.insertOne({t_id: 5, name: "Pooja", dept: "IT", salary: 25000, status: "not approved"})

🔍 Queries
1️⃣ Find the list of teachers in IT Department
db.Teachers.find({dept: "IT"})

2️⃣ Find the list of teachers who have salary greater than 50000
db.Teachers.find({salary: {$gt: 50000}})

3️⃣ Find the teacher’s list in descending order of salary
db.Teachers.find().sort({salary: -1})

4️⃣ Remove the teacher whose status is not approved
db.Teachers.deleteMany({status: "not approved"})

5️⃣ Give an increment of ₹20000 to teachers having salary less than 30000
db.Teachers.updateMany({salary: {$lt: 30000}}, {$inc: {salary: 20000}})
