🔍 Step 3: Execute 10 Queries
🧩 (a) $where Queries
1️⃣ Find employees where salary > 40000 (using $where)
db.Employees.find({$where: "this.salary > 40000"})

2️⃣ Find employees where age is greater than salary divided by 2000
db.Employees.find({$where: "this.age > this.salary / 2000"})

3️⃣ Find employees whose name starts with “S” using $where
db.Employees.find({$where: "this.name.startsWith('S')"})

🧭 (b) Cursors (Limit, Skip, Sort, Options)
4️⃣ Display first 3 employees only (limit)
db.Employees.find().limit(3)

5️⃣ Skip first 2 records and display next ones (skip)
db.Employees.find().skip(2)

6️⃣ Sort employees by salary in descending order
db.Employees.find().sort({salary: -1})

7️⃣ Sort employees by department (ascending) and salary (descending)
db.Employees.find().sort({dept: 1, salary: -1})

8️⃣ Limit and sort combined – top 2 highest-paid employees
db.Employees.find().sort({salary: -1}).limit(2)
