15. Create Instructor collection and execute following queries
a. Find out details of instructors working in IT department
b. Find out details of instructors who have salary>40000
c. Give count of inst in comp dept
d. Give incrementof 10000 to instructor whose status is approved

Step 1: Create / Select Database
use CollegeDB

🧱 Step 2: Create Collection
db.createCollection("Instructors")

👨‍🏫 Step 3: Insert Sample Data (One by One)
db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 1, name: "Omkar", dept: "IT", salary: 35000, status: "approved"})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 2, name: "Sakshi", dept: "CSE", salary: 48000, status: "approved"})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 3, name: "Rohan", dept: "COMP", salary: 42000, status: "approved"})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 4, name: "Sneha", dept: "IT", salary: 60000, status: "not approved"})

db.Instructors.insertOne({inst_id: 5, name: "Pooja", dept: "COMP", salary: 30000, status: "approved"})

🔍 Step 4: Execute Queries
a️⃣ Find details of instructors working in IT department
db.Instructors.find({dept: "IT"})

b️⃣ Find details of instructors who have salary > 40000
db.Instructors.find({salary: {$gt: 40000}})

c️⃣ Count of instructors in COMP department
db.Instructors.countDocuments({dept: "COMP"})

d️⃣ Give increment of ₹10,000 to instructors whose status is approved
db.Instructors.updateMany({status: "approved"}, {$inc: {salary: 10000}})
